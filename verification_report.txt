14. Weaknesses an interviewer could attack
   1. The subset is small: 1 of 3 states and 1 of 3 categories, about 19% of
      M5's 30,490 series.
   2. Single level only: there's no hierarchy or reconciliation across store,
      department or total.
   3. The test window is short: 16 weeks in early 2016 with only 4 folds. It has
      no holiday season and no confidence intervals or significance test on the
      3% edge over ETS.
   4. No tuning, early stopping or fixed seed, so the LightGBM result can't be
      replicated exactly.
   5. Future price and events are assumed known. That's realistic for M5 but not
      for every retailer, and price_ratio at h>1 depends on it too.
   6. No external features: no weather, no specific event types, no stock-out
      signals. Zero sales could be a stock-out rather than zero demand, which
      isn't handled.
   7. The newsvendor costs are invented, and the comparison doesn't pad the point
      forecast with safety stock.
   8. No automated leakage test. The leak-free design follows from how the
      windows are written, not from a test.
